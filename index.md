# Privacy Policy for ThinkToScroll

**Last updated: October 2026**

## Overview

ThinkToScroll ("the App") is a screen time management app developed by Sankalpa Apps. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

## Data Collection

ThinkToScroll does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. The App has no backend, no accounts, and no analytics.

## Data Stored on Your Device

All data created by the App is stored **locally on your device only** and never leaves it:

- App usage limits you configure
- Todo tasks you create
- Task completion history
- App settings and preferences

This data is stored in your device's local storage (SQLite database and SharedPreferences) and is only accessible by the App.

## Permissions Used

- **Usage Stats** — to monitor time spent in selected apps and enforce your set limits
- **Display over other apps** — to show the blocking overlay when a limit is reached
- **Foreground Service** — to run the background monitor that tracks app usage

None of these permissions are used to collect or share data.

## Third-Party Services

ThinkToScroll does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

ThinkToScroll is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future update, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
